about

Built by students,
for students.

STEM Discovery Launchpad started as a frustration — science classes felt disconnected from actual science. So we built the thing we wished existed.

the mission

Most STEM programs are about covering curriculum. We're about something different — building a place where students do real work, ask uncomfortable questions, and actually ship things.

Whether you're pipetting DNA in a lab or debugging a Python script at midnight, the Launchpad is your space to go deeper than a classroom will let you.

"We didn't want another program that looks good on a resume. We wanted one that changes how you think."
how it started
2022

The frustration

Three students. One science fair. A lot of "why isn't there a real community for this?"

2023

First cohort

8 members, a bioinformatics track, and a Discord server. Scrappy but real.

2024

Grew to 60+ members

Added robotics, chemistry, and data science tracks. Projects started getting published.

now

120+ members, 6 cohorts

Still student-run, still free, still figuring it out as we go.
